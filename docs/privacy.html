Market Intel Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Overview

Market Intel is a personal financial research, market analysis, and paper-trading application operated for private use.

Google Account Data

Market Intel uses Google OAuth authorization to access Gmail for the limited purpose of sending automated Market Intel notifications to the application's owner.

Market Intel does not use Gmail access to read, modify, delete, or analyze email messages.

Data Sharing

Google account data obtained through Market Intel is not sold, shared with advertisers, or provided to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials used by Market Intel are stored privately and are not included in the application's public source-code repository.

Revoking Access

Google account access granted to Market Intel can be revoked at any time through the security settings of the associated Google account.

Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy may be directed to clay.marketintel@gmail.com.
